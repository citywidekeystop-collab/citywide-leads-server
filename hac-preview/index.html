Rural Policy

Policy Priorities

HAC’s 2026 rural housing policy agenda focuses on expanding affordable housing opportunities in rural communities.

Housing supply

Increase the availability and affordability of rural homes.

Local capacity

Strengthen rural housing and community development organizations.

Access to credit

Expand safe, affordable lending options in rural communities.

Rental preservation

Protect USDA multifamily housing as existing mortgages mature.

Federal resources

Maintain and adapt federal housing programs for rural residents.

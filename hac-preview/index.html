Home / Lending Products

Financing at every stage

Lending Products

HAC offers financing for rural housing projects from feasibility through long-term operation. Review eligibility and terms with HAC before applying.

Submit a loan inquiry ↗Official loan terms and products ↗

Predevelopment

Design, engineering and feasibility expenses.

Acquisition

Purchase land, buildings or existing housing.

Construction

Site work, construction and rehabilitation.

Permanent / Mini-Perm

Longer-term financing for rental property operations.

Line of Credit

Revolving capital for experienced developers.

SHOP / Self-help housing

Specialized loans for eligible self-help homeownership projects.

Rental housing preservation

Financing and support for preserving affordable rental properties.

Housing Assistance Council

About HAC

Founded in 1971, HAC is a national nonprofit dedicated to improving housing conditions for people with low incomes in rural communities, especially those facing the greatest hardship.

Our Mission

Affordable housing and stronger communities through capital, training, research and policy.
